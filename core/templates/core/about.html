About BrizZ's Team

Crafting refined and powerful Android experiences.

Welcome to BrizZ, a project dedicated to providing a clean, stable, and highly customizable Android operating system. Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that you should have complete control over your device. We build upon the solid foundation of AOSP or HyperOS, infusing it with thoughtful features and performance enhancements that empower our users without sacrificing the simplicity and speed of stock Android.
